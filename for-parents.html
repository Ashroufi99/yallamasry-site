For parents

A little language.
A lasting connection.

Simple, thoughtful play for families who want their children to hear Egyptian Arabic and enjoy learning together.

Family-first learning

Made for little hands and curious minds.

Short, easy interactions let children explore at their own pace. Parents can sit beside them, repeat the words, and carry the conversation into everyday life.

No ads. No accounts.

Children can play without signing in and without advertising or tracking.

Works offline.

Animal images and voices are built into the app; internet access is not needed during play.

Gentle encouragement.

Positive voice feedback, stars, and thoughtful screen-break reminders support calmer use.

Our approach

“Learning a family language begins with hearing it, enjoying it, and sharing it.”
