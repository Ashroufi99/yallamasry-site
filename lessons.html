Hear about new lessons

We are working on more ways for little ones to learn Egyptian Arabic. If you’d like, we can send you an email when something new arrives.

What we’re planning

Ideas we are exploring include:

  • Colors
  • Numbers and letters
  • Egyptian food
  • Family words
  • Greetings
  • More learning games

These are plans, not promises, and we don’t have release dates yet.

Get an email

Signing up is optional and is for adults only. It is separate from the children’s app: the app never asks for an email address.

What happens next

  • We’ll email you a link to confirm. Your address is added only after you tap it.
  • We send occasional news about new lessons and app updates, nothing else. We don’t track whether you open our emails or click their links.
  • Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.
  • Our list is kept by Buttondown, the email service we use. We never sell your email. See our Privacy Policy.
