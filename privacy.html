Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Publisher: Ashraf Mostafa · Contact: hello@yallamasry.app

In short: Yalla Masry does not collect any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

What the app does with information

  • We collect nothing. The app does not ask for or send your name, email, location, photos, microphone, contacts or device identifiers.
  • Everything stays on the device. All pictures and sounds are inside the app. The only thing the app remembers is the number of stars earned, saved on your own device. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.
  • No third parties. The app contains no advertising, analytics, social media or tracking tools, and it does not link to other websites.
  • No accounts. The app needs no sign-in. The current version is free to download and has no in-app purchases. If a future version offers optional paid lessons, purchases will be handled by Apple behind a parent-only check, and we will update this policy before that version is released.

Children

Yalla Masry is made for young children to use with a parent or guardian. Because the app collects no information, there is nothing to collect from children, and nothing to share or sell. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for parental consent where the law requires it.

Recordings

The voices in the app are recorded with the permission of the people who speak, or their parents or guardians. They are played from inside the app and are not captured from you.

Changes

If the app’s practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@yallamasry.app
