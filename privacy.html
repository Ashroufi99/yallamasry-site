Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Published by Ashraf Mostafa · hello@yallamasry.app

In short: the Yalla Masry app does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection. If a parent chooses to record their own voice for the games, those recordings stay on that device and are never sent to us or anyone else. When your child plays Repeat after me, the app listens for their voice on the device and keeps nothing. This policy also explains our website and its optional email updates, which are separate from the app.

1. Who we are

Yalla Masry is made and published by Ashraf Mostafa. Questions about privacy are always welcome at hello@yallamasry.app.

2. Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask for, collect or send any personal information: no names, email addresses, photos, contacts, location or device identifiers. Nothing the app records ever leaves your device. It never uses the camera.

Microphone, only if a parent chooses. The optional “Record your voice” feature (on the first language screen, and in For Parents) lets a parent record the app’s short spoken lines in their own voice, so the games speak to the child in that voice. It opens only after a grown-up question, and the microphone is used only while the parent is recording. iOS asks for permission first, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Yalla Masry › Microphone.

Microphone in Repeat after me. In this game the app says a word and then listens for a few seconds while your child repeats it. The sound is used on the device only to tell that your child has had a go, then thrown away straight away. It is never saved, sent or shared, and no one hears it. The microphone is on only while the game is listening, when the screen says “أنا سامعك · I’m listening”. If you turn the microphone off in Settings, the game still teaches each word without listening.

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no third-party tracking tools and no in-app purchases. All of its pictures and sounds are stored inside the app, so it does not need the internet to work.

3. Information kept on your device

The app remembers how many stars have been earned, which language and voice you chose, and any recordings a parent made with “Record your voice”. These are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the app. A parent can also delete their recordings at any time from the “Record your voice” page.

4. Children’s privacy

Yalla Masry is made for young children, ideally playing alongside a parent or caregiver. Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing collected from children to use, share or sell. Children cannot reach the recording feature: it opens only after a grown-up question, and it records the parent, not the child. Repeat after me listens for the child’s voice only to know they have had a go; the sound stays on the device, is not saved or sent, and so nothing is collected.

The voices in the app were recorded with the permission of the people speaking, or of their parents or guardians. Recordings a parent makes with “Record your voice” are plain audio of that parent reading the app’s lines, kept only inside the app on that device. They are not uploaded, shared or analysed, and no voice is copied, cloned or generated from them.

5. Third-party services

The app uses no third-party services. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes (see GitHub’s documentation). The website does not use advertising or analytics. The optional email updates described below use Buttondown.

6. Email updates from our website

This is separate from the app and is for adults only. On our New Lessons page, a grown-up can choose to give us their email address and, optionally, which languages their family is interested in.

Buttondown, the email service we use, stores this list and sends the emails on our behalf. You will first receive an email asking you to confirm. We have switched off tracking of whether our emails are opened or links clicked. Buttondown may still keep basic technical information, such as the IP address used to sign up, to run and protect its service (see Buttondown’s privacy policy). We use the list only to send occasional news about new lessons and app updates. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers that help us send these emails.

7. Keeping and deleting information

Stars, your language and voice choice and any parent recordings stay on your device until you delete them or the app. Email addresses stay on our update list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can write to us at any time to have your address deleted.

8. Your choices and contacting us

You don’t need to share anything to use the app. For any question or request about privacy, including deleting your email address, write to hello@yallamasry.app.

9. Changes to this policy

If anything about how Yalla Masry handles information changes, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a future version offers optional paid lessons, purchases will be made through Apple behind a grown-up check, and this policy will be updated first.

10. Effective date

This policy is effective from November 7, 2026.
