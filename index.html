Egyptian Arabic for little ones

Yalla Masry is a gentle app for iPhone and iPad where young children hear everyday Egyptian Arabic, meet friendly illustrated animals and learn by listening and play.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

Meet the animals

Tap any of the illustrated animals to hear its name in Egyptian Arabic, from أرنب (rabbit) to نمر (tiger), each with its letter of the alphabet.

Find the Card

A friendly voice asks «فين البطّة؟» (“Where is the duck?”) and your child picks the right picture from three cards, earning stars along the way.

Real Egyptian voices

Every word is spoken in everyday Egyptian Arabic, the way families talk at home, so children hear the sounds and rhythm of the language.

Made for families

  • No advertisements, no tracking and no account to create.
  • Works offline: all pictures and voices are inside the app.
  • Free to download, with no in-app purchases.
  • Gentle reminders to take a screen break after a longer session of play.

More on the way

We are working on more ways to learn, such as colors, greetings and family words. Get an email when new lessons arrive.
