New in 1.4

Itkalim: hear it, trace it, count it.

Yalla Masry is now Itkalim (اتكلم, “speak”), with three games on one simple carousel:

  • Let’s play. Hear “فين الجمل؟” and pick the right animal from three big cards. The name appears only after the right pick, so the answer is never given away.
  • Trace the letters. Follow the glowing dots of each letter with a finger, and feel a soft tap from the phone at every dot.
  • Airport numbers. Tap a number and hear it, then watch the split-flap board count up to it, click by click.

Choose Egyptian Arabic or Turkish when you start. For Egyptian Arabic, pick Ashroufi’s or Ranoona’s voice.

Your voice, kept at home.

Parents can record the game’s short lines in their own voice, so your child hears you ask “فين الجمل؟” and cheer “برافو!”. Pick “Record your own voice · سجّل صوتك” on the first screen, or find it any time in For Parents. Any line you skip stays in the family voice you picked.

Nothing leaves your phone. Your recordings stay on your device. They are never uploaded, shared or used to make other voices, and deleting the app deletes them. Recording opens only after a grown-up question. Read our Privacy Policy.
