Say it out loud.

In “Repeat after me · قولي ورايا”, each animal says its word, then it’s your child’s turn. The screen says “قول أسد!”, the game listens, and the moment your child says it the card turns green with a big “برافو!”. Every try counts.

Your voice, kept at home.

Parents can record the game’s short lines in their own voice, so your child hears you ask “فين الجمل؟” and cheer “برافو!”. Pick “Record your own voice · سجّل صوتك” on the first screen, or find it any time in For Parents. Any line you skip stays in Ashroufi’s warm Egyptian voice.

Nothing leaves your phone. Your recordings stay on your device. They are never uploaded, shared or used to make other voices, and deleting the app deletes them. Recording opens only after a grown-up question. In Repeat after me the app listens for your child’s voice on the phone and keeps nothing. Read our Privacy Policy.

You can also choose your child’s language when you start. Egyptian Arabic is ready now, with more Arabic dialects and world languages coming soon.
