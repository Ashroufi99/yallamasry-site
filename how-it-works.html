How it works

Small moments. Big discoveries.

Three gentle ways to meet first words in your family’s language, whichever language that is: find them, trace them, and count them.

01

Find

In “Let’s play”, hear “فين الجمل؟” and pick the right animal from three big cards. No reading needed, and the name shows only after the right pick.

02

Trace

Follow the glowing dots of each letter with a finger. The phone gives a soft tap at every dot, and each letter says its name.

03

Count

On the airport board, tap a number from one to ten, hear it, and watch the split-flap board count up to it, click by click.

Inside the experience

Real pictures.
Real voices.

Every word is spoken by a real voice: for Egyptian Arabic, Ashroufi’s or Ranoona’s, or yours if you record the lines yourself. Children explore at their own pace, with stars and warm encouragement along the way.

The experience is easy to share with a parent or grandparent—listen together, point together, and practice words away from the screen.
