Yalla Masry Support

Thank you for learning Egyptian Arabic with Yalla Masry!

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email hello@yallamasry.app. Please include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version. Please do not send photos, recordings or other personal details of children.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or internet? No. Yalla Masry needs no sign-in and works offline.

Is it free? Yes. Yalla Masry is free to download, has no ads, and everything in it today is free. Later versions may offer optional extra lessons, which only a grown-up can buy.

How do I hear a word again? Tap the animal card, or the speaker button in the game.

The sound is off. Check that the silent switch is off and the volume is up. Yalla Masry plays through the speaker like any other app.

My stars disappeared. Stars are saved only on the device, so deleting the app or restoring a new phone resets them.

Which devices work? iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.
