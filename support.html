Questions and answers

What is Itkalim?

An app that helps young children discover the languages of the world, starting with Egyptian Arabic, Turkish and Canadian English (it was called Yalla Masry when it taught Egyptian Arabic only). They play “Let’s play”, a listening game where they choose the animal that matches what they hear, trace the letters with a soft tap at every dot, count from one to ten on a flipping airport board, explore their body in “My body”, and travel from Earth to the Milky Way in “Our Universe”.

Which devices can use it?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.

Does it need the internet?

No. After you download it, Itkalim works fully offline. All pictures and voices are stored inside the app.

How do we hear a word or question again?

In “Let’s play”, tap the speaker button next to the question to hear it again. In letter tracing, tap the letter. On the airport board, tap the number again.

There is no sound, or it is very quiet.

Itkalim plays sound even when the Ring/Silent switch is set to silent, so you don’t need to change it. Instead:

  • Turn the volume up with the side buttons while the app is open.
  • Check that headphones or a Bluetooth speaker aren’t connected somewhere else.
  • Close Itkalim completely and open it again.

What is Our Universe?

A 3D world in three views, chosen with the buttons at the top. Earth and Moon shows day and night and the Moon’s phases; zoom out to see the seasons, and drag the Earth along its path around the Sun. Solar system shows the planets on their real paths as they travel with the Sun. Milky Way replays the Sun’s 220-million-year orbit around the centre of the galaxy; tap a spiral arm to learn about it. Tap the Sun, the Moon or any planet to see its name. Pinch or use the + and − buttons to zoom.

Are the planets in the right places?

Yes. The Sun, Moon and planets are placed for the real date, calculated on the device. Sizes and distances are enlarged so little eyes can see them; the app says so on screen.

Is there a sign-in?

Children never need to sign in. A parent can choose to sign in with Apple, after a grown-up question; it is optional, and the name and email stay on the device. To remove it, go to For Parents — للأهل › Your account › Delete account.

How do stars work?

Your child earns a star for each correct answer in “Let’s play”, and a game is complete after ten stars. Stars are saved only on your device, so they start again if you delete and reinstall the app or move to a new device.

Why did a break message appear?

After about 15 minutes of active play, Itkalim gently suggests taking a break from the screen. You can choose to keep playing together.

How do I report a bug?

Email hello@itkalim.app with your device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened.

Can I record my own voice?

Yes. On the first language screen, choose “Record your own voice · سجّل صوتك”, or later go to For Parents — للأهل › Your voice. After a quick grown-up question, read each short line aloud and your child hears you in the games. Lines you skip keep the family voice you picked. Recordings stay on your device and are never uploaded; you can delete them on the same page. If recording does not start, turn on Settings › Itkalim › Microphone.

What about privacy?

The app collects no personal information: no account is needed, and there are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Will there be new lessons?

Yes, we are working on more ways to learn. We can’t promise dates yet. Leave your email if you would like to hear when they arrive.
