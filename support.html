Questions and answers

What is Yalla Masry?

An app that helps young children discover Egyptian Arabic. They tap illustrated animals to hear their names and play “Find the Card”, a listening game where they choose the picture that matches what they hear.

Which devices can use it?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 15 or later.

Does it need the internet?

No. After you download it, Yalla Masry works fully offline. All pictures and voices are stored inside the app.

How do we hear a word or question again?

On the home screen, tap any animal card to hear its name. In “Find the Card”, tap the speaker button next to the question to hear it again.

There is no sound, or it is very quiet.

Yalla Masry plays sound even when the Ring/Silent switch is set to silent, so you don’t need to change it. Instead:

  • Turn the volume up with the side buttons while the app is open.
  • Check that headphones or a Bluetooth speaker aren’t connected somewhere else.
  • Close Yalla Masry completely and open it again.

How do stars work?

Your child earns a star for each correct answer in “Find the Card”, and a game is complete after ten stars. Stars are saved only on your device, so they start again if you delete and reinstall the app or move to a new device.

Why did a break message appear?

After about 15 minutes of active play, Yalla Masry gently suggests taking a break from the screen. You can choose to keep playing together.

How do I report a bug?

Email hello@yallamasry.app with your device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened.

What about privacy?

The app collects no personal information: no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Will there be new lessons?

Yes, we are working on more ways to learn. We can’t promise dates yet. Leave your email if you would like to hear when they arrive.
