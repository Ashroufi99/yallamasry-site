Questions and answers

What is Itkalim?

First words for little ones in Egyptian Arabic, Turkish and English: find the animal, trace letters, count, My body and Our Universe.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is inside the app.

What is Our Universe?

Earth and Moon, the seasons, the planets and the Milky Way in 3D. Tap anything to hear its name. Planets are placed for today’s date.

Is there a sign-in?

Children never sign in. Parents can sign in with Apple if they want; it stays on the device. Remove it in For Parents › Your account › Delete account.

No sound?

Turn the volume up while the app is open, check headphones or Bluetooth, then close and reopen Itkalim.

Can I record my own voice?

Yes: For Parents › Your voice. Recordings stay on your device. If it won’t record, turn on Settings › Itkalim › Microphone.

Found a bug?

Email hello@itkalim.app with your device, iOS version and what happened.

Privacy?

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.

New lessons?

Yes, more are coming. Leave your email.
